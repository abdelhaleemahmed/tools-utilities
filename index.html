slimv

Available · v0.2.0

Shrink video course libraries to H.265/HEVC without visible quality loss. An ffmpeg-driven CLI that analyzes a library, benchmarks encoders on your own footage, re-encodes, and verifies every output before you delete a thing.

  • 🧠 VMAF benchmarking on your real content
  • ⚡ Intel Quick Sync / NVIDIA NVENC acceleration
  • 👁️ eyeball & downscale-test to judge quality
  • ✅ Full-decode verify gate before deleting sources
